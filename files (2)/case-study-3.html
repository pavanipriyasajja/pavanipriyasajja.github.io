Case Study 03

E-Commerce

Reimagining online shopping for a sustainable fashion brand

Client EcoWear
Role Lead Designer
Timeline 5 Months
Year 2024
Website Showcase

Overview

EcoWear is a sustainable fashion brand committed to ethical manufacturing and environmental responsibility. They needed an e-commerce platform that would not only sell their products but tell their story and educate customers about sustainable fashion. The goal was to create a shopping experience as thoughtful as their products.

The Challenge

Sustainable fashion brands face unique challenges in e-commerce:

💰

Price Perception

Ethical products cost more, requiring clear value communication.

📖

Story Telling

Need to convey complex sustainability information without overwhelming.

🛒

Conversion

Previous site had 1.2% conversion rate, well below industry average.

Research & Discovery

We conducted comprehensive research to understand the sustainable fashion shopper:

12

Customer interviews with existing and potential customers

3

Usability tests on the existing website to identify friction points

1000+

Session recordings analyzed using heatmaps and click tracking

Key finding: Customers wanted transparency about materials and manufacturing, but the existing site buried this information in hard-to-find pages.

Homepage Design
Product Pages
Checkout Flow

Design Approach

01

Content Strategy

Restructured content to integrate sustainability information naturally throughout the shopping experience.

02

Visual Direction

Created an organic, nature-inspired aesthetic with earthy tones and plenty of whitespace.

03

Product Pages

Designed "Transparency Tabs" showing materials, factory info, and carbon footprint for each item.

04

Checkout Optimization

Streamlined checkout from 5 steps to 2, with progress indicators and guest checkout.

Key Features

Impact Dashboard

Shows customers the environmental impact of their purchases—water saved, carbon offset, and materials recycled.

Transparency Tabs

Every product page includes detailed information about materials, manufacturing, and the people who made it.

Size Predictor

AI-powered tool that recommends sizes based on body measurements and fit preferences, reducing returns.

Circular Program

Integrated trade-in and recycling program allowing customers to return worn items for store credit.

Technical Implementation

The site was built on Shopify Plus with custom theme development and integrations:

Shopify Plus Liquid JavaScript Klaviyo Loop Returns Fit Analytics

Results

3.4% Conversion rate (up from 1.2%)
42% Increase in average order value
35% Reduction in return rate
2.5x Time on site increase
"The new website doesn't just sell our products—it tells our story. Customers now understand and appreciate the value of sustainable fashion, and it shows in our metrics." — Sarah Chen, CEO of EcoWear
