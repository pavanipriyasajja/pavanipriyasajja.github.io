Case Study 02

Mobile App

Designing a mindfulness app for better mental wellness

Client MindFlow
Role UX/UI Designer
Timeline 6 Months
Year 2024
App Showcase

Overview

MindFlow is a startup focused on making mental wellness accessible to everyone. They needed a mobile app that would help users develop daily meditation and mindfulness habits through a calming, intuitive experience. The app needed to stand out in a crowded market while remaining approachable for beginners.

The Challenge

The mental wellness app market is saturated, and user retention is notoriously low. Our key challenges were:

🧘

User Engagement

Most meditation apps see 90% drop-off within the first week.

🎯

Personalization

Users have vastly different needs, from stress relief to better sleep.

✨

Differentiation

Standing out against established players like Calm and Headspace.

Research

We conducted extensive user research to understand what drives long-term engagement with wellness apps:

15

User interviews conducted with both beginners and experienced meditators

500+

Survey responses analyzed to identify pain points and preferences

8

Competitor apps analyzed for features, UX patterns, and gaps

Key finding: Users wanted to feel a sense of progress and community, not just consume content passively.

Wireframes

Design Process

01

User Research

Interviews, surveys, and competitor analysis to identify opportunities.

02

Information Architecture

Defined user flows, navigation structure, and content hierarchy.

03

Wireframing

Created low-fidelity wireframes for key screens and interactions.

04

Visual Design

Developed a calming visual language with soft gradients and organic shapes.

05

Prototyping

Built interactive prototypes for user testing and stakeholder review.

06

User Testing

Conducted usability tests and iterated based on feedback.

Solution

The final design features a unique "Journey" system where users progress through themed meditation paths, unlocking new content as they build their practice. Key features include:

Adaptive Onboarding

A personalized quiz that creates a custom meditation plan based on goals and experience level.

Progress Visualization

Beautiful, animated progress indicators that celebrate consistency and milestones.

Community Challenges

Weekly group challenges that create accountability and connection without social pressure.

Smart Reminders

AI-powered notifications that learn the best times to prompt users based on their behavior.

Results

4.8 App Store rating
67% Day-7 retention rate
100K Downloads in first month
12min Average daily session
