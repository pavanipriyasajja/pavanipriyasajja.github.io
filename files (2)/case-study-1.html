Case Study 01

Brand Redesign

Revitalizing a heritage brand for the digital age

Client Heritage Corp
Role Lead Designer
Timeline 4 Months
Year 2023
Project Hero Image

Overview

Heritage Corp, a 50-year-old manufacturing company, approached us with a challenge: their brand no longer resonated with modern audiences while remaining connected to their legacy. The goal was to create a visual identity that honored their heritage while positioning them as an innovative leader in their industry.

The Challenge

The existing brand identity was created in the 1980s and hadn't evolved significantly since. Key challenges included:

📊

Outdated Perception

Customers viewed the brand as old-fashioned and resistant to change.

🎨

Inconsistent Identity

No design system existed, leading to fragmented brand expressions.

💻

Digital Deficit

The brand lacked any meaningful digital presence or strategy.

Process

01

Discovery

Conducted stakeholder interviews, competitor analysis, and brand audits to understand the current state and opportunities.

02

Strategy

Developed brand positioning, messaging framework, and creative direction based on research insights.

03

Design

Created logo concepts, color palette, typography system, and initial applications.

04

Systems

Built comprehensive brand guidelines and design system for consistent implementation.

Logo Exploration
Color System
Typography

Solution

The new brand identity maintains a connection to the company's heritage through refined typography and a modernized logo mark that references their original 1974 design. The color palette evolved from their traditional navy to a sophisticated slate gray with energetic accent colors, symbolizing their evolution while respecting their roots.

A comprehensive design system was created with 200+ components, ensuring brand consistency across all touchpoints from business cards to their new website.

Results

45% Increase in brand recognition
60% More website engagement
3x Social media growth
28% Increase in leads
